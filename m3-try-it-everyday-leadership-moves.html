Everyday Leadership Moves: A Self-Audit

How you'd like to work:

Part 1 of 5

About this activity

You have examined culturally responsive leadership through research, a leadership challenge, and relational practice. Now turn the lens toward your own repeated behaviors.

This self-audit is not a scorecard. It is a way to notice where your stated values are already visible, and where your routines may be communicating something different.

What you'll do

Review nine statements across three areas: communication, decision-making, and relationships. Respond based on what people are most likely to experience from you now — not what you intend to do on your best day.

The scale

  1. 1 — Rarely true of my current practice
  2. 2 — Sometimes true
  3. 3 — Often true
  4. 4 — Consistently true

There is no total at the end. Nothing is added up, and no number means anything on its own.

This is private to you. Your responses stay in this browser. Nothing is submitted, transmitted, or visible to a facilitator or anyone in your cohort. They are kept on this device only, and you can download or clear them at any time.

If you are on a shared or public computer, use Clear my responses before you leave the page.
